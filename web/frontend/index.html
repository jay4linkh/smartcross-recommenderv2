Inside the engines

The real pipeline, stage by stage. Select a stage to see exactly what it did to the profile currently loaded.

Scoring happens before filtering. Filtering first would bias the probabilities; tilting last would promote products the customer cannot have. The hard constraint runs last so nothing downstream can re-admit an ineligible product.

How well it ranks

Measured on the last three months, held out entirely. The model only ever saw earlier months — including when the lift matrix was computed.

What the data actually says

Every reason the console gives traces back to one of these measured sequence rules. Lift is how much more often a product is taken by people who already hold another, against its own base rate.
